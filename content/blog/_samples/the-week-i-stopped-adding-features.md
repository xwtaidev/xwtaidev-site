I set aside a week to finish the edges of a little app. No new capabilities. No experiments. Just the work I had been stepping around while building the interesting parts.

## A small observation

I removed a setup screen, combined two settings, and rewrote the first empty state. The app became easier to explain because it had fewer things asking for an explanation. It was also easier to test, which was a welcome side effect.

> Sometimes the next version needs less.

## The next small step

The next time a project feels tangled, I want to make a list of what could disappear. There may still be a missing feature. But there is often an extra decision first.
