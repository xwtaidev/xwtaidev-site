While sketching the products page, I kept returning to a folder. It is an ordinary object with an unusually clear promise: there are a few related things inside, and you can open it.

## A small observation

The visual metaphor did some of the explaining for me. Cards could belong together without becoming a table. Opening the folder could become the transition into exploring them. The important part was making the movement support that understanding, rather than decorating it.

> Familiar things leave more room for unfamiliar ideas.

## The next small step

I want to keep testing these small physical references. A good one can make an interaction feel obvious before anyone reads the instructions.
