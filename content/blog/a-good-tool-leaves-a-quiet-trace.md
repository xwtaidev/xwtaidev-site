The features I appreciate most rarely announce themselves. A note opens where I left it. A task stays on the right day. An export produces exactly the files I expected.

## A small observation

These details are difficult to put in a launch screenshot. They are also the reason I keep using a tool after the launch has passed. Reliability becomes part of the background, leaving attention free for the work itself.

> A little less friction, every ordinary day.

## The next small step

I want to spend more time on that background. Not because the visible parts do not matter, but because the small promises are the ones a person encounters every day.
