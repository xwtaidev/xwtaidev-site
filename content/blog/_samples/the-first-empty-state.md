Most of my development happens with a comfortable pile of test data. Notes have titles, boards have cards, and everything looks occupied. It is easy to forget that nobody else starts there.

## A small observation

An empty state has a very small job: explain where you are and give you a useful next action. A large illustration is optional. A clear starting point is not. I try to use the same words here that I would use sitting beside someone trying the tool.

> An empty screen is still a first impression.

## The next small step

Before the next release, I will open a completely new workspace and follow the first minute slowly. The empty version often has the most useful feedback.
