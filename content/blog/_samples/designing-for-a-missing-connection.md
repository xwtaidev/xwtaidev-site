Disconnecting the network has become one of my favorite ways to learn about a tool. It quickly reveals which parts are actually local, which actions are queued, and which promises the interface cannot keep.

## A small observation

A useful offline state distinguishes between saved, waiting, and failed. These are different situations and deserve different words. The screen should also tell you what you can still do, rather than simply announcing what went wrong.

> A missing connection should not erase a clear next step.

## The next small step

For the next sync feature, I want the disconnected version in the first sketch. That is when the model is still easy to change, and when recovery can become part of the normal path.
