There is a particular kind of satisfaction in finishing a small tool. It does one job, you understand every corner of it, and there is nothing left on the list except to use it.

## A small observation

I often spoil that moment by imagining what it could become. A platform. A subscription. A second tab full of features. Sometimes those ideas are worth following. But the original problem was usually much more modest, and solving it was the whole point.

> What if this is already enough?

## The next small step

I am trying to leave a finished idea alone for a week before adding anything. Use is a better source of direction than the excitement of a fresh roadmap.
