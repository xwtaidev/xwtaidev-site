A first version used to mean a lot of placeholders to me. The navigation suggested a future product, while only one or two buttons actually did anything. It looked like progress, but it was hard to use.

## A small observation

Now I prefer one complete screen. Put something in, do the useful thing, get the result, and come back tomorrow. That loop reveals practical problems much earlier than an impressive collection of disconnected views.

> One complete loop is a good beginning.

## The next small step

For the next prototype, I will write down the first real task someone should finish. Everything on the screen should help that task happen, including the way it ends.
