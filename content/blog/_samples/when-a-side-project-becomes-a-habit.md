The projects that survive are usually the ones I can return to in a normal evening. Not a perfect uninterrupted weekend. Just a little time after everything else, with a clear place to begin.

## A small observation

Leaving that place matters. I try to stop with a useful note about the next change, rather than a vague sense that the project needs more work. A small starting point makes the habit easier to keep.

> An ordinary evening is enough to keep going.

## The next small step

I want to measure progress by the number of times I return with interest. A project does not need to occupy every spare hour to become something worth sharing.
