When an idea feels particularly urgent, I try to write it down before creating a new project. What is happening now? What should become easier? What would the smallest useful change look like?

## A small observation

The words do not need to be polished. They just need to be specific enough to disagree with. Sometimes the explanation gets longer as I write, which is a useful sign that I do not yet understand the scope.

> Sometimes the first useful version is a sentence.

## The next small step

I am keeping these little descriptions beside the code. They make it easier to notice when an interesting implementation has drifted away from the problem that made me start.
