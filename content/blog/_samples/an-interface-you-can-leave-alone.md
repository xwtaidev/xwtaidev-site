I have been trying to make a workspace quieter. Not emptier, exactly. Just a place where the important thing stays important, even when there is a lot going on around it.

## A small observation

The first useful change was hiding the controls I only needed occasionally. The second was giving unfinished work a clear home. Neither required a new feature. Both made the next action easier to see. A tool can offer a lot without asking you to consider all of it at once.

> Good tools know when to get out of the way.

## The next small step

For the next version, I want to notice where I hesitate. If I stop to interpret a screen before doing the thing I came for, there is probably another decision the interface can make on my behalf.
