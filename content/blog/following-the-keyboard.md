There is a point in nearly every interface where the keyboard reveals something the mouse let me ignore. Focus moves to the wrong place. A dialog closes and leaves me nowhere. A control has no name.

## A small observation

Walking through a task without a pointer is a practical design review. It makes the order of actions explicit. It also asks whether the screen is explaining its state clearly enough to work without the little clues of a hover.

> If the focus gets lost, so does the thought.

## The next small step

I am keeping a short keyboard walkthrough next to the release checklist: enter, create, edit, cancel, recover, leave. It is a small amount of work that catches surprisingly important details.
