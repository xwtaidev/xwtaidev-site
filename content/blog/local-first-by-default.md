A local file is a surprisingly good foundation for trust. You can see it, copy it, back it up, and open it with another tool. Those simple properties matter more to me as my notes grow older.

## A small observation

Local-first software still has difficult engineering problems. Sync, conflict resolution, and recovery need care. But starting with an accessible local copy changes the failure mode. Losing a connection does not have to mean losing the ability to work.

> Your files should outlast the tool.

## The next small step

For a new feature, I now ask what remains available when the network disappears. That question has been a useful way to find the real boundary between the tool and the service around it.
